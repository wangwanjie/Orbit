安装Install

不确定芯片类型？打开「苹果菜单 › 关于本机」，看“芯片”或“处理器”一栏。Not sure which chip you have? Open › About This Mac and check “Chip” or “Processor”.

  1. 下载与本机芯片匹配的 DMG，打开后把 Orbit 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。Download the DMG for your chip, open it and drag Orbit into Applications.
  2. 从「应用程序」中打开 Orbit，它会出现在菜单栏，并打开控制中心。Launch Orbit from Applications. It appears in the menu bar and opens the Control Center.
  3. 在控制中心的“权限”页按需授予辅助功能、屏幕录制、完全磁盘访问等权限。Grant Accessibility, Screen Recording, Full Disk Access and other permissions as needed on the Permissions page.
  4. 访达右键菜单需要在「系统设置 › 通用 › 登录项与扩展 › 访达扩展」中启用 Orbit Finder。To use the Finder context menu, enable Orbit Finder in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Finder.
